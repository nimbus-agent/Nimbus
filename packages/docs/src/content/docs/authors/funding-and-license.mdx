---
title: Funding and license
description: Declare funding links and an SPDX license in your extension manifest, and how users reach the links with nimbus extension sponsor.
---

Two optional fields in `nimbus.extension.json` say how to support you and under
what terms the code is offered.

```json
{
  "funding": [{ "url": "https://github.com/sponsors/acme", "label": "GitHub Sponsors" }],
  "license": "MIT"
}
```

- **`funding`**: 1 to 8 entries of `{ "url", "label" }`. The `url` is required:
  `https` with a host, no user name or password, at most 2048 characters. The
  `label` is optional, 1 to 64 characters. No two entries share a `url`.
- **`license`**: an SPDX license expression such as `MIT` or
  `Apache-2.0 OR MIT`, at most 128 characters.

## Checks

A malformed field is never a runtime error: every gateway ignores it and
`extension info` shows `(invalid, ignored)`. But `sign` refuses it (exit `2`,
`manifest <field> is invalid: <reason>; fix it before signing`) and `publish`
refuses it with `ERR_REGISTRY_MANIFEST_INVALID`, so a bad field cannot reach a
registry through Nimbus's own tools. Nimbus never verifies that a link works or
that a license is true.

## What users see

```bash
nimbus extension sponsor acme/notion-tasks
```

`sponsor` prints every link and, on a terminal, asks `Open <host> in your
browser? [y/N]` before opening one (`--index <n>` picks which). Nimbus takes no
payment and sees none. The desktop app shows no funding yet.

If someone clones your extension with `--publisher`, your `funding` links are
removed from their copy, so they cannot be mistaken for theirs.
